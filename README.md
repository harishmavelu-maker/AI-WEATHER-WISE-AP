# AI-WEATHER-WISE-AP
Weather predictions
AI WEATHER WISE AP
                 An AI-powered weather intelligence and personal weather assistant designed to provide weather information, forecasts, alerts, and simple recommendations.
Problem Statement 

                   People often find it difficult to understand changing weather conditions and plan their daily activities based on complex weather information. A simple AI-powered system can provide weather details in an easy-to-understand format.
Objective

.              Build a simple web application where users can enter a location and get current weather information, forecasts, weather alerts, and AI-based suggestions.
Features

Weather Search: Search weather information by city or location.
Current Weather: Displays temperature, humidity, wind speed, and weather conditions.
Forecast: Provides upcoming weather information.
AI Weather Explanation: Explains weather conditions in simple language.
Weather Alerts: Provides important warnings for severe weather conditions.
Daily Suggestions: Gives suggestions based on the weather.
Location-Based Weather: Get weather information for a selected location.
Simple Interface: Easy-to-use interface for students and general users.
Tech Stack

Python, Streamlit, Weather API, Google Gemini API, GitHub
